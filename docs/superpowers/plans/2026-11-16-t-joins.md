# T-joins and BuildPlan Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `geometry.BuildPlan` builds a whole plan, including walls that end against another wall (T-joins): the partition is cut flush to the face and the through wall loses the covered strip from its net face area. The wasm builder becomes an adapter over it.

**Architecture:** New file `geometry/plan.go` holds the plan types, `BuildPlan` and IDs. `geometry/join.go` holds the join checks, the trim and the strips, reusing the existing `wallSpan` (a trimmed end is a mitre: `otherStart`/`otherEnd` move) and `wallLayers`/`splitLayers`. `internal/wasmapi/build.go` maps JSON to `Plan` and `Built` back to JSON.

**Tech Stack:** Go (no cgo, os or net in geometry), Node tests for the wasm files.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-t-joins-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Synthetic fixtures only. No private names in code, tests, docs or commits; the staged-diff grep gates every commit with `&&`.
- Signed Conventional Commits, never `--no-verify`. The PR is squash-merged with the title `feat(geometry): build walls that meet mid-wall (T-joins)`, which cuts a minor release.
- geometry imports no cgo, `os` or `net`.
- Existing builders keep their signatures and results. `BuildPlan` with no joins returns exactly what they return.
- Tolerance is `minDim` (1 mm). Angle range 15°–165° inclusive.
- Element IDs: `w1`, `w1/0`…, `r1/0`…, `r1/0/0`…, `s1`, `s1/0`…. Item IDs: 1–64 of letters, digits, `_ . : -`, unique across the plan.
- Caps: 10,000 items, 100,000 elements, strips count toward a wall's 256-opening cap.
- New codes: placement reason `in_join`; join reasons `unknown_wall`, `not_on_face`, `too_shallow`, `off_wall`, `joins_overlap`.

## Review Focus

1. A partition whose end lies exactly on the through wall's *end* (a corner, not a T): refused `off_wall`, never a zero-width or negative strip.
2. A partition taller than, or starting below, the through wall: the strip is the vertical overlap only.
3. A chain at 45° where the untrimmed face would accept a second join but the trimmed face does not: refused `off_wall`.
4. Room through wall: a strip on the opposite face near an outside corner is checked against the mitred span, not `[0, length]`.
5. NaN, Inf or huge coordinates in a joined plan: no panic, bounded work (fuzz).
